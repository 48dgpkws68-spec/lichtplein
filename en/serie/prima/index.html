Liregus Alfa

The Alfa range by Liregus, European manufacturer of wiring accessories since 1944: reliable essential wiring accessories for construction and renovation, with and without frame, flush and surface, in white and sand.

Universal and comfortable

Alfa is the broad essential series by Liregus, wide in range and in colour. The visible parts are extra scratch resistant, self-extinguishing polycarbonate that is antistatic and does not discolour under UV light. All Alfa products fit flush frames from 1 to 5 gangs and surface frames from 1 to 5 gangs. That makes Alfa the logical choice for housing, rental homes and renovation.
