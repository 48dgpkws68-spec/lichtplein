Description

Back-lit LED panel 30x120 cm with the LEDs behind the diffuser: even light without a light guide plate and a higher efficacy than edge-lit. White aluminium frame of 35 mm, suitable for suspended ceilings with a 30x120 cm grid, as surface mount with the matching frame or suspended with the wire kit. Choose the version, wattage, colour temperature and glare class on this page (UGR<22 for general areas, UGR<19 for offices with screen work). Driver and 20 cm supply cable included. Packed per carton, delivered on a pallet from Dutch stock.
