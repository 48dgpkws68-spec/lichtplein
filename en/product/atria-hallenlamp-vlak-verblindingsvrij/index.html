Description

Flat LED high bay with very low glare (UGR<21) and a soft 110 degree beam. With 160 lm/W, IK10 impact resistance and an aluminium housing it suits warehouses, production halls and sports halls where comfort matters. Dimmable via 0-10V (DALI version available separately); leave the dim wires unconnected for simple on/off use.

  • UGR<21, no glare
  • 160 lm/W
  • IK10 impact rated
  • 0-10V dimmable
  • 5 year warranty
