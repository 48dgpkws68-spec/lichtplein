Description

Edge-lit LED panel 60x60 cm with a slim 9 to 12 mm housing and a PMMA light guide plate that does not yellow. White aluminium frame, suitable for suspended ceilings with a 60x60 cm grid, as surface mount with the matching frame or suspended with the wire kit. Choose the version, wattage, colour temperature and glare class on this page. Driver and supply cable included. Packed per carton, delivered on a pallet from Dutch stock.
