Description

Aluminium lighting column with 6 metre light point height, stepped or conical. All aluminium, so no coating or paint needed, no corrosion and 100 percent recyclable. The wide base houses electronics and withstands impacts. 60 mm spigot for our street luminaires. Natural finish or RAL coated on request; foundation and root section available.

  • All aluminium, low maintenance
  • Stepped or conical
