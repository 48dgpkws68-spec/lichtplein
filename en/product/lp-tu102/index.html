Description

Conical steel lighting column to the Dutch standard with a 4 metre light point height and a 800 mm root section (total length 4.8 m). Made of S235JR steel, 3 mm wall, taper 14 mm/m, 60 mm spigot for all our street and post-top luminaires. Hot-dip galvanised to DIN EN ISO 1461, then powder coated in RAL 9005 black for a clean streetscape. Door 85 x 400 mm at 500 mm above ground with triangular lock and C-rail for the connection box, two 50 x 150 mm cable entries in the root. For residential streets, car parks, business parks and cycle paths. Laser-welded longitudinal seam, CE marked to DIN EN 40. Options on request: other RAL or DB colour, base plate to prevent sinking, edge protection for the cable entry and a heat-shrink sleeve as extra corrosion protection at ground level.

  • Light point height 4 m
  • Dutch standard column, CE to EN 40
  • Hot-dip galvanised inside and out
  • 85 x 400 mm door with lock
  • 60 mm spigot for our street lights
