Description

Round LED tubular luminaire of 900 mm and 30 W in an anodised aluminium housing, powder coated in RAL 7004 grey. Sealed design with IP67 and IK10 (tested to IEC 60598-1), made for poultry and livestock houses, tunnels, underpasses and car parks. More than 100 lm/W, colour temperature 3000K, 4000K or 5000K to choose, CRI above 80 and a lifetime of more than 100,000 hours. Dimmable via 1-10V, PWM or an internal potentiometer, so suitable for lighting programmes in livestock houses. Operates from -40 to +50 degrees and on 90 to 305 V. Mounted with a bracket to ceiling or wall. Price on request, delivered per project.

  • IP67 and IK10, DEKRA tested
  • 30 W, more than 100 lm/W
  • Dimmable 1-10V, PWM or internal
  • From -40 to +50 degrees
