Description

Timeless LED post-top luminaire in a diamond shape with a nano-opal cover of UV-stabilised polycarbonate. The symmetrical optic gives friendly light with good facial recognition, 6 or 16 W (1,162 to 2,632 lumens) in 3000K or 4000K. Very efficient (up to 229 lm/W), IP67 and IK10, protection class II and dimmable. Fits columns with a 60 mm spigot and is intended for light point heights of 4 to 5 metres in residential areas, parks and along cycle and footpaths. Housing in RAL 9005 black. Fully circular design, 10 year warranty. Price on request, lead time by arrangement.

  • 1,500 lm at 3000K
  • Symmetrical optic, opal cover
  • IP67 and IK10
  • 60 mm spigot, light point height 4 to 5 m
  • 10 year warranty
