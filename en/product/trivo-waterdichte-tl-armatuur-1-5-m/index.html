Description

Waterproof LED tri-proof luminaire (IP65) in polycarbonate with an internal aluminium heat sink. Screw-free opening and linkable, so long runs install quickly. Versions with fixed wattage, adjustable wattage and CCT, DALI-2 or built-in microwave sensor. For car parks, stables, car washes and cold stores.

  • IP65 waterproof
  • Linkable
  • Screw-free mounting
  • DALI-2 and sensor versions
  • 5 year warranty
