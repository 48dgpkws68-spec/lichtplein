Columns

Lighting columns for every project

Conical steel lighting columns of 4 and 6 metres, aluminium columns and charging lanterns, all at a sharp price on request. All matching our street and post-top luminaires, delivered to the project address.

TURRIS

Steel columns, 4 and 6 metres

Hot-dip galvanised, optionally powder coated in RAL 9005. Price on request, answer within 48 hours.

View the steel columns →

Dutch standard

Conical columns to EN 40 with 60 mm spigot, 85 x 400 mm door and root section. Fit all our street and post-top luminaires.

Hot-dip galvanised

S235JR steel, 3 mm wall, galvanised to DIN EN ISO 1461. Optionally powder coated in RAL 9005 or another project colour.

Sharp price on request

Add the columns to your quote with the required quantity and you receive a sharp price within 48 hours. For a complete project we quote a project price including luminaires.

Delivered per project

Columns are delivered by truck to the project address, together with the luminaires and foundation material if required.

How to order columns

  • Choose height and finish and add the columns to your quote; you receive a price within 48 hours.
  • Combine with a street or post-top luminaire from the VIA and POLARIS lines.
  • Foundation sleeves, ground cables and connection boxes are supplied on request.
  • Lead time for columns: 3 to 5 weeks, by truck to the project address.
