Sint Jozefkerk, Achterveld: classic lighting around the church square

Around the Sint Jozefkerk in Achterveld, classic lanterns with LED technology light the church square safely and atmospherically without overpowering the historic building. What a church square needs in light, and how you recreate it with the classic post-top luminaire from POLARIS.

A church at the heart of the village

Achterveld is a village in the municipality of Leusden, on the border between the provinces of Utrecht and Gelderland. The Sint Jozefkerk is its natural centre: on the square in front of it visitors park, people linger after a service and the village gathers. A square like this needs lighting that suits the building by day and gives a safe, calm atmosphere after dark.

The picture around the church: classic lanterns with modern LED technology, street furniture beside a historic façade by day, directed light on the square after dark without casting a harsh glare on the church. Lichtplein did not carry out this project; the square serves here as an example of what this combination does.

Classic outside, LED inside

With a classic LED lantern, the canopy and the slender body set the look and the LED module sets the performance: around 140 lm/W, tens of thousands of hours and a beam that stays directed downwards, so spill light towards the façade and the surrounding homes stays limited.

Around a church that brings an extra advantage: the lanterns light the square, not the building. If you want to accent the façade, do it separately with a warm floodlight, so both layers can be switched independently.

What a church square needs in light

In practice a church square is a pedestrian area with a parking function. As a rule of thumb, expect an average of 5 to 10 lux with good uniformity; the walking routes to the entrance and the parking bays decide where the columns go. A light point height of 4 metres is usual for a classic lantern.

With 4,000 lumens per lantern at 4 metres, column spacing usually lands between 12 and 20 metres, depending on the shape of the square. A lighting calculation on the actual column positions prevents a dark corner at the entrance.

How to recreate it with POLARIS

If you want the same look, the classic post-top luminaire from POLARIS is the match: 40 W, 4,000 lumens at 140 lm/W, 4000 K, IP66, a 120 degree beam, an 80 mm spigot and up to 5 years warranty. If you prefer warmer light, the modern POLARIS luminaires are adjustable from 2200 to 4000 K.

You choose the column to go with it: TURRIS supplies conical steel columns of 4 and 6 metres from stock, galvanised or powder-coated in RAL 9005, with a NEXUS cut-out box in the door opening. A compact floodlight from FLUX in 2700 K lights the façade separately.

What to check when choosing

  • Spigot. The classic POLARIS takes 80 mm; measure existing columns first.
  • Colour temperature. The classic version gives 4000 K; 3000 K or warmer often suits brick better and comes from the modern POLARIS.
  • Ingress protection. IP66 keeps out rain, road salt and the pressure washer.
  • Lead time. Luminaires within 5 working days, steel columns in 3 to 5 weeks: order the columns first.
  • Quantities. From 20 columns or 100 luminaires a project price applies; Lichtplein sells to businesses only.

Frequently asked questions

Did Lichtplein light the Sint Jozefkerk itself?

No. The church square in Achterveld serves as an example of what classic lanterns do beside a historic building. Lichtplein supplies the comparable classic post-top luminaire from POLARIS, columns and cut-out boxes to installers and municipalities.

Which colour temperature suits a church square?

Beside brick and natural stone, warm light of 2200 to 3000 K looks most natural. The classic POLARIS gives 4000 K; the modern versions are adjustable from 2200 to 4000 K.

How many lux belong on a church square?

Expect an average of 5 to 10 lux with good uniformity, a little more at the entrance and the parking bays. A lighting calculation gives certainty on your own column positions.

Can I light the church façade with the same lanterns?

No, the lantern directs its light downwards. For a façade accent, use a separate compact floodlight from FLUX in a warm colour, so the square and the façade remain switchable independently.

How long does delivery take?

Luminaires within 5 working days from European stock, steel columns 3 to 5 weeks. Cut-out boxes and aluminium columns on a project basis, price on request.
