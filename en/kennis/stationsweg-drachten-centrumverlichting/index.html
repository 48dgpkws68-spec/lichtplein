Stationsweg, Drachten: robust lighting in the town centre

In the heart of Drachten, a robust street luminaire lights the Stationsweg: low consumption, little glare and a timeless design. What this project teaches for similar town-centre streets, and how the VIA series from Lichtplein fits that brief.

A town-centre street with many users

The Stationsweg runs through the heart of Drachten. As in most town-centre streets, everything comes together here: shops and hospitality, residents above the premises, cyclists, pedestrians and early-morning deliveries. The lighting has to serve all of them without dominating the streetscape.

The choice fell on a clean, timeless street luminaire mounted on a column. Three requirements come first in a street like this: a robust housing, low energy consumption and as little glare as possible for shoppers and residents.

Robust in practice

In a town-centre street, robust means more than a sturdy canopy. The luminaire sits for years in rain, road salt and exhaust fumes, and takes the occasional knock during events or deliveries. In concrete terms that comes down to:

  • IP66: dust-tight and protected against water jets, no condensation in the optic.
  • IK08 or higher: resistant to knocks from tail lifts, scaffolding and balls.
  • 10 kV surge protection: a thunderstorm or a switching spike on the grid does not cost you a driver.
  • Replaceable driver and LED module: maintenance from the access platform, without dismantling the column.

Little glare, enough light

A shopping street generally falls within the P classes of EN 13201, in the order of 7.5 to 15 lux average horizontal, depending on footfall and the sense of safety. The road authority sets the class. That level is generally achievable with 25 to 50 W of LED per light point at a mounting height of 4 to 6 metres, depending on column spacing and optic.

You limit glare with a flat, shielded lens and a tilt angle of 0 degrees, so that no light escapes above the horizon. A warm white colour temperature of 2700 or 3000 K looks calmer than 4000 K and suits facades and terraces better.

Low consumption all night long

Compared with high-pressure sodium or compact fluorescent, LED often saves half or more in streets like this, depending on the existing installation. A dimming profile that drops to 50 percent late at night saves a substantial further share of the annual consumption, without the street feeling dark. A driver with DALI-2 or an astronomical dimming clock handles that without extra cabling.

How the VIA series fits this brief

This project was not carried out by Lichtplein, but anyone lighting a similar town-centre street will find the same principles in the VIA series: street luminaires from 25 to 100 W in IP66, with 10 kV surge protection, tiltable on a 60 mm spigot and up to 160 lm/W. You choose the driver: DALI-2 with D4i for municipal asset management, or adjustable output via dip switch for a straightforward installation.

Matching columns come from the TURRIS series: conical steel lighting columns of 4 and 6 metres, hot-dip galvanised or powder-coated in RAL 9005. The NEXUS column cut-out fits in the door opening and provides the fuse for each column.

Frequently asked questions

What light level suits a town-centre street?

Often a P class according to EN 13201, in the order of 7.5 to 15 lux on average. The road authority sets the class and a lighting calculation determines the level per street.

Which colour temperature suits a shopping street?

2700 or 3000 K. Warm white light looks calmer, renders facades and terraces naturally and causes less glare than 4000 K.

Does UGR also apply to street lighting?

No, UGR is a measure for interiors. Outdoors you limit glare through the luminaire's luminous intensity class, a flat lens and a tilt angle of 0 degrees.

Does a VIA luminaire fit an existing column?

Yes, if the spigot is 60 mm. The luminaire is tiltable, so you set the angle to 0 degrees at installation for minimal glare.

How long do columns and luminaires take to deliver?

Luminaires within 5 working days from European stock, steel columns in 3 to 5 weeks. Project prices apply from 100 luminaires or 20 columns.
