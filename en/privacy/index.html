Privacy statement

What data we process

For quote requests, account registration and orders we process company details (company name, registration and VAT number) and contact details (name, email address, phone number, delivery address).

What we use it for

To prepare quotes, execute agreements, invoicing, delivery and customer service. With your consent we also inform you about new products and promotions.

Retention

We keep data no longer than necessary. Fiscal data is kept for 7 years as legally required.

Sharing with third parties

Data is only shared with parties needed to execute the agreement (such as carriers, the form service and payment providers), and never sold.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Contact us at info@lichtplein.nl.

Cookies

We only use functional browser storage (quote list and language choice). No tracking cookies are set.
